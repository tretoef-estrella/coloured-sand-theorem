# Licence

This repository holds two kinds of work, and each has the licence that suits it.

| What | Licence |
|---|---|
| **Code**: every engine and script (`.py`) | **MIT** — the text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| **Texts**: the paper, the sealed predictions, the logs and every other document | **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) |

## What CC BY 4.0 allows, and what it asks

You may copy, redistribute, translate, adapt and build upon these texts, for any purpose, including commercially. In return you must give appropriate credit — name the author, **Rafael Amichis Luengo** (cited as *Amichis Luengo, R.*) —, link to the licence, and say whether you changed anything, without suggesting that the author endorses your use.

## What MIT allows, and what it asks

You may use, copy, modify, merge, publish and distribute the code freely, provided the copyright notice and the permission notice in [LICENSE](LICENSE) are kept in every copy or substantial portion of it.

## Scholarly use

If a result, an idea or a number from this repository is used in research — whether or not any text or code is copied — please cite the paper.

## What is not covered

The papers of other authors that were read are **not** in this repository; they are only cited. The texts are provided **as is**; the authoritative mathematical statements are those of the paper.
